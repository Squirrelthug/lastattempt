Long

{{ site.name }} is an independent World of Warcraft media project. It began in February 2026 as the {{ site.show_name }}, a weekly show hosted by {{ site.author }} with co-hosts and guests from the Last Attempt community. The show's premise is simple: cover the week in Warcraft (news, patch notes, developer communication, the Race to World First, Mythic+ and PvP esports, and community discussion) through the eyes of people who are living it every raid night.

{{ site.name }}'s editorial approach is to separate what Blizzard has confirmed, what the hosts think, what the community is saying, and what nobody knows yet. It aims to contextualize Warcraft information rather than repeat announcements, and to be candid about the difference between understanding a system and having personally theorycrafted it.

{{ site.name }} is a community first: an open Discord, an in-game community, and a Guild Spotlight on the site that features guilds of every kind, progression, social and stream-anchored, rather than promoting one. The podcast is published on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube and other platforms, with written articles at lastattempt.net. {{ site.name }} is not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment.
