Last Attempt started as a way to talk about our guild's week: what we killed, what we didn't, what changed in the patch notes on Tuesday and what it meant by Thursday. Thirty-two episodes later it's still that — but it's also become the place where I keep up with Warcraft more closely than I ever did as a "casual" player, and BlizzCon made it obvious that a weekly two-hour audio file is not the only shape that should take.

So: a website. Not because a podcast needs one, but because the things I want to do next — written commentary on the announcements, the systems questions WoW Forever is about to create, the occasional interview — don't fit in a Spotify description box.

!!! know "What this site is"
    The podcast, every episode with show notes and audio. Articles, starting with BlizzCon 2026. A press kit and a contact page, because if we're going to ask people for their time we should be easy to find and easy to describe.

!!! think "What it isn't"
    A news wire. Wowhead and the official site will always beat us to *what* happened. What we're here for is *what it means* — to someone who has raided every tier since Shadowlands, follows the class Discords, and knows the difference between understanding a system and having personally simmed it.

!!! open "What's coming first"
    The BlizzCon 2026 series: WoW Forever and what "forever" actually commits Blizzard to, the Skyborne, hundreds of new items dropping into an ecosystem everyone thought was solved, Midnight: Eclipse, and The Last Titan for those of us who started in Northrend.
