## Privacy

This site is a set of static pages. It does not use cookies, does not have accounts, and does not sell or share personal data.

- **Analytics.** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookie-less and does not track individuals across sites. It tells us which pages are read and roughly where visitors come from.
- **Audio and embeds.** Episode audio is served from our podcast host (Spotify), and some pages embed the Spotify player. Those services may set their own cookies and are governed by their own privacy policies.
- **Fonts.** Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts.
- **Email.** If you email us, we keep the message so we can reply. We don't add you to anything.

## Corrections

We correct mistakes. If we got something wrong, tell us at <hello@lastattempt.net> and we'll fix it and note the change on the page.
